### Statewide and federal

- NWS Fort Worth, Dallas/Fort Worth climate narrative (July and August highs consistently in the 90s, often 100+; spring and fall wettest; severe weather peaks in spring; hail, damaging winds and flooding nearly every year): https://www.weather.gov/fwd/dfw_narrative
- OSHA 29 CFR 1926.453 aerial lifts (daily control tests, authorized operators, lanyard to boom or basket, no belting off to adjacent structures): https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-B/chapter-XVII/part-1926/subpart-L/section-1926.453
- OSHA 29 CFR 1926.602(d) (construction forklift operator training follows 1910.178(l)): https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-B/chapter-XVII/part-1926/subpart-O/section-1926.602
- 14 CFR 77.9 (FAA notice above 200 ft AGL, or exceeding a 100:1 surface out to 20,000 ft from runways over 3,200 ft): https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-E/part-77/subpart-B/section-77.9
- FAA Form 7460-1 (file at least 45 days before start; covers temporary structures such as cranes): https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Form/FAA_Form_7460-1_052026.pdf
